About the C Lab Course

The Laboratory C 20465 course at the Open University is a challenging but rewarding course that teaches students the fundamentals of C programming and system-level programming concepts.

This section of my website contains resources, guides, and tips based on my experience with the course, where I achieved a perfect score of 100 on the final project.

Whether you're struggling with MacBook compatibility issues, looking for project tips, or just trying to understand course requirements better, you'll find helpful information here.
